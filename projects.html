Scam & Phishing Detector

Live

Problem — Text-based scams and phishing links are difficult to detect, and most existing tools are not tuned to how scam messages are actually written.

Solution — A text classifier for scam messages, paired with a separate URL/phishing classifier to evaluate both the message and the link.

Built with — TF-IDF and Logistic Regression for text classification, trained on a custom 5,000-row dataset of Indian-context scam messages, achieving 96% recall on spam. A Random Forest model handles URL/phishing detection using PhiUSIIL features.

DYNAMIS Fitness

Live

Solution —DYNAMIS brings fitness tracking into one focused platform, letting users log workouts, track body measurements and weight, monitor progress photos, manage supplements and view their training progress from a single account.
